    Common questions

    Anything else, write to hello@tamarindlane.example.

    How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

    Orders are roasted on Tuesday and shipped by Thursday. Most customers receive their bag within two to four days of roasting.

    Do you grind the coffee?

    Yes. Choose whole bean or a grind size at checkout. We recommend whole bean if you have a grinder, because ground coffee goes flat faster.

    Can I change my coffee between deliveries?

    You can switch coffee, bag count or frequency until Monday before each roast. Changes apply from the next delivery.

    Where does the coffee come from?

    Dark Lantern comes from a cooperative in Brazil, Monsoon Hill from an estate in Chikmagalur, and First Light from a washing station in Ethiopia. Each bag lists the producer on the label.
